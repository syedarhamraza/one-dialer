# Name and icons

The source code of One Dialer is licensed under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE). The GPL covers the code, not the brand.

The following are **not** licensed for use in forks or redistributed builds:

- the name "One Dialer" and "One Dialer Recorder"
- the app icons and logo (`docs/play-icon.png`, the launcher icons in `android/app/src/main/res/`, and `assets/app_icons/`)
- the application ids `com.syedarhamraza.onedialer` and `com.syedarhamraza.onedialer.recorder`

If you publish a fork, on Google Play or anywhere else, give it a different name, different icons and a different application id, so users can tell it apart from the official app. Building the app for yourself, unchanged, is fine.

You may say your fork is "based on One Dialer" and link back here.

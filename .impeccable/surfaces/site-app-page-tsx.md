Scope: the public landing page at syedarhamraza.github.io/one-dialer/. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: Android owners deciding whether to replace their phone app, mostly arriving on a phone.
Action: join the closed beta (two steps), later one Google Play button (`RELEASED` in `app/links.ts`).

## Direction contract

THESIS: One phone-sized slab of call glass turns as you scroll and the page's words bend through it. Refuses the split hero of copy beside a framed phone over a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: The app's Frosted Night Glass. Pitch black ground, a slow violet and green aurora, white Geist at poster scale, one refractive squircle slab with a lit rim, glass pills for controls, flat hairline rows for content, one lit green action per screen.

STORY: The visitor sees what the app is made of, turns it through five real screens, tries the keypad and the effects tiers on the page itself, then joins the beta in two steps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: "Calls, in glass." in two lines at about 300px from the left edge, "glass." in green. A tall glass slab (about 86% of the viewport height) floats right of centre, tilted, refracting the letters behind it and tilting to the pointer. Bottom left: one sentence and the lit green Join the beta. Bottom right: the five screen names, the current one white. Top: brand left, three links right.

# South Asia budget/midrange smartphone market and Unisoc SoCs (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, 2019–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-06. About 15 searches and fetches. Counterpoint's and IDC's own pages were reached only through press coverage. Model-to-chip mappings marked **[UNVERIFIED]** come from the researcher's background knowledge and were not confirmed against a spec database in this session. Check them on gsmarena before relying on them.

## Market share by brand and by chipset vendor

### Takeaway
India is a vivo/Samsung/OPPO/Xiaomi/realme market. Counterpoint puts MediaTek at about 45–53% of shipments and Qualcomm at about 25–31% (2023–2025), with Unisoc and Exynos in the remaining ~20%. Unisoc's India share is never broken out. Pakistan and Bangladesh are Transsion markets (Infinix/Tecno/itel lead), which means far more Unisoc and low-end MediaTek. Sri Lanka is Samsung-led by web usage.

### Inferences
- About 20% of Indian shipments (roughly 30M phones a year) run on Unisoc, Exynos or Google Tensor. Apple is a separate ~7–10% that isn't Android. Unisoc is probably the bigger part of that at the sub-₹10k end (Samsung Galaxy A0x, Transsion, realme C, Lava), but this is inferred, not measured.
- Pakistan and Bangladesh have a far higher Unisoc and low-end Helio (G25–G85) share than India, because Transsion leads both markets and its entry lines (itel, Smart, Spark Go, Pop) are largely Unisoc.
- Statcounter (installed base, web traffic) and IDC (new shipments) give different pictures. Statcounter better reflects what users of this app actually hold, including older phones.

### Gaps
- No India-specific Unisoc or Exynos share for any year was found. None at all for Pakistan, Bangladesh or Sri Lanka.
- No Counterpoint, IDC or Canalys shipment data for Sri Lanka, and no PTA brand data beyond local assembly.
- No vendor share for 2019–2022 was retrieved (MediaTek overtook Qualcomm in India around 2020–21; not verified).

## Best-selling models by year and their SoCs

### Takeaway
Few model-level rankings are public. The confirmed best sellers are the Redmi 9A (Q1 2021), Galaxy A14 5G (2023), and Redmi 13C, vivo T2x, Galaxy A15 and vivo Y28 (Q1 2024 5G). Transsion's best sellers in India in Q4 2020 were the TECNO Spark 6 Air, itel Vision 1 and Infinix Smart 4 Plus. The chips are mostly MediaTek Helio and Dimensity, with Unisoc in the entry Transsion, realme C and Galaxy A0x lines.

## Unisoc SoCs: identifiers, GPU, benchmark, phones

### Takeaway
Unisoc's Android platform codenames are well documented (ums512 = T610/T618/T700; ums9230 = T606/T612/T616/T7200-series; ums9620 = T760/T770/T820; ums9621 = T750/T765/T8200; sc9863a = SC9863A). On-device `ro.board.platform` is expected to be the lowercase codename but was not confirmed per chip. GPU benchmarks were found only for the Mali-G57 MP1 class.

### Cited Findings
- Codename → marketing name: sc9863a → A8581 (the SC9863A). ums312 → T310. ums512 → T610, T618, T700, A7862. ums9230 → T606, T612, T616, T72xx. ums9620 → T760, T770, T820, S8000, A7870, T8100, T9100. ums9621 → T750, T765, T8200. ums9360 → T7300. ums9632 → T8300, T9300. ud710 → T710, T740, T7510 — [TomKing062 bootloader wiki](https://github.com/TomKing062/CVE-2022-38694_unlock_bootloader/wiki/AddSupportToModel) (community source)
- The kernel DTS confirms `sprd,ums512` / `sprd,ums512-1h10` and `sprd,sp9863a-1h10`, plus UMS9620 support — [LKML UMS512](https://lkml.iu.edu/hypermail/linux/kernel/2303.0/04666.html); [LKML UMS9620](https://lkml.iu.edu/2312.1/09794.html)
- Real getprop logs on other Spreadtrum chips show the lowercase codename (`[ro.board.platform]: [sp9832e]`, `sp9853i`) — [newandroidbook Alldocube log](https://newandroidbook.com/ddb/Alldocube)
- Notebookcheck also calls the T8200 "T765", the T7250 "T615" and the T7280 "T620" — [NBC T8200](https://www.notebookcheck.net/UNISOC-T8200-T765-Processor-Benchmarks-and-Specs.785130.0.html); [NBC T7250](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Unisoc-T7250-T615-Processor-Benchmarks-and-Specs.929759.0.html); [NBC T7280](https://www.notebookcheck.net/UNISOC-T7280-T620-Processor-Benchmarks-and-Specs.930518.0.html)
- Chip specs (Android Authority, June 2022). All 12 nm T6xx parts are 2×Cortex-A75 + 6×Cortex-A55, 4G:
  - SC9863A: 8×Cortex-A55 1.6 GHz, PowerVR GE8322, 28 nm, 4G. Phones: realme C11 2021, Galaxy A03 Core.
  - T606: Mali-G57 MC1 in the spec table but "G52 MC1" in the text. Phones: Moto E32, Nokia G22, Galaxy A03.
  - T610: Mali-G52 MC2. Phone: realme C25Y.
  - T612: Mali-G57 MC1. Phone: Nokia T21.
  - T616: Mali-G57 MC1.
  - T618: Mali-G52 MC2.
  - T760/T770: 6 nm, 5G, Mali-G57.
  - Source: [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/unisoc-processors-guide-848691/)
- Notebookcheck German page lists the T606 GPU as Mali-G52 MP2. The T606 GPU is still disputed across sources — [Notebookcheck DE](https://www.notebookcheck.com/T606-vs-T612-vs-T618_14018_14386_12997.247552.0.html)
- T7250: Mali-G57 MP1, AnTuTu 381k or 496k (91mobiles contradicts itself), Geekbench 441/1480 — [91mobiles T7250](https://www.91mobiles.com/processor/unisoc-t7250-pdp)

### Inferences
- For a tier list, match on the codename (`ums9230`, `ums512`, `sc9863a`/`sp9863a`, `ums9620`, `ums9621`, `ums312`) as well as the marketing name. Several marketing names share one codename: T606, T612, T616 and T72xx all report as ums9230, so the codename alone can't tell them apart. They sit in one GPU class anyway (Mali-G57 MP1, ~400–560 Wild Life Unlimited). T610/T618/T700 (ums512, Mali-G52 MP2) are similar or slightly weaker. SC9863A (PowerVR GE8322) is clearly the lowest tier and should be treated like Helio A22/P35.
- `Build.HARDWARE` on Unisoc is expected to be the board name (e.g. `ums512_1h10`, `ums9230_4h10`) and `Build.SOC_MANUFACTURER` "Spreadtrum" or "Unisoc"; neither was verified. Prefix matching on `ro.board.platform` is the safe approach.

### Gaps
- No primary source (a stock-phone `getprop` dump, or the Unisoc site) confirmed the `ro.board.platform`, `Build.SOC_MODEL` or `Build.HARDWARE` strings per chip. Needs `adb shell getprop | grep -iE "platform|soc|hardware"` on a real T606/SC9863A phone.
- The Notebookcheck comparison page returned 403. No per-chip 3DMark or GFXBench figures for SC9863A/GE8322, Mali-G52 MP2 (T610/T618/T700), T760, T7250 or T8200.
- The Unisoc official product pages were not fetched.

## Typical RAM and Android version (share below API 31)

### Takeaway
Statcounter India (mid/late 2026 snapshots) shows Android 11 at about 9% and Android 12 at about 7–10%. Android 10 and older weren't in the top rows found. Roughly 10–20% of Indian Android web traffic is likely still below API 31 (Android 12), so `Build.SOC_MODEL` will be missing on them.

### Cited Findings
- Statcounter India: Android 11 at 8.81% and 12 at 9.63% (June 2026); 11 at 9.29% and 12 at 6.97% (Sept 2026); 13 at 14.0% and 14 at 12.4% (July 2026); 15 at 23.3% and 16 at 24.1% (Aug 2026) — [Statcounter Android version India](https://gs.statcounter.com/android-version-market-share/mobile-tablet/india)

### Inferences
- Android 11 (~9%) plus Android 10 and older (not captured, likely several % more) puts about 10–20% of devices below API 31. Pakistan and Bangladesh probably skew older, because of itel/Smart phones and Android Go editions.
- Typical RAM [UNVERIFIED, researcher knowledge]: SC9863A-era phones had 2–3 GB (Android 10/11 Go). T606/T612 phones had 3–4 GB, plus "virtual RAM". 2024–26 sub-₹10k phones have 4–8 GB.

### Gaps
- No sourced RAM distribution. No Android-version data for Pakistan, Bangladesh or Sri Lanka, and no 2025 India snapshot (only 2026 pages were cached).

## Price bands defining budget vs midrange

### Takeaway
IDC bands India in USD: sub-$100 and $100–200 ("mass budget", 41% of 2025 shipments), $200–400 ("entry premium", 26%), $400–600 ("mid premium", 5%). No primary source for INR bands was retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- IDC 2025 India: mass budget (<$200) fell 8% YoY, from 44% to 41% of shipments; leaders vivo, OPPO, Motorola. Entry premium ($200–400) fell 5%, from 28% to 26%. Mid premium ($400–600) grew 23% to 5%. ASP was a record $282 — [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/indian_smartphone_market_remains_flat_in_2025_signals_structural_changes-news-71575.php)
- Counterpoint 2025: about 1 in 5 Indian shipments was premium — [GSMArena](https://m.gsmarena.com/counterpoint_1_in_5_smartphones_shipped_in_india_in_2025_were_premium_models-news-71366.php)
- Counterpoint and press coverage commonly use the ₹20–30k band (e.g. OnePlus Nord, OPPO F19 Pro+ as band leaders) — [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/india-smartphone-market-q3-2020-1172785)

### Inferences
- Working bands: budget < ₹10k (about $120); affordable ₹10–20k; midrange ₹20–35k; premium > ₹30–45k. These match the press usage above but are not a sourced standard. Pakistan and Bangladesh skew lower: IDC Bangladesh calls a whole segment "ultra-low-end".

### Gaps
- No Counterpoint INR price-band definitions or band shares were retrieved. No PKR, BDT or LKR price-band sources.
